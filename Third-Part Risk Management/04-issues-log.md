## 1. SOC 2 Exceptions in User Access Review Testing

**Category:** ![Compliance Issue](https://img.shields.io/badge/category-Compliance%20Issue-6f42c1) &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Severity:** ![High](https://img.shields.io/badge/severity-High-e36209) &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Status:** ![Open](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-Open-d73a49)  
**Owner:** Augustine Ozor &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Due Date:** 2025-11-30

**Description**  
PaySecure's SOC 2 Type II report notes exceptions where periodic user access reviews were not consistently performed or evidenced during the audit period, creating risk of stale or excessive access within the cardholder data environment. Vendor has been asked for a corrective action plan and evidence that quarterly access reviews have resumed; re-verification is planned at the next annual SOC 2 cycle.

## 2. Outdated Penetration Test Evidence

**Category:** ![Security Gap](https://img.shields.io/badge/category-Security%20Gap-d73a49) &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Severity:** ![Medium](https://img.shields.io/badge/severity-Medium-dbab09) &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Status:** ![Open](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-Open-d73a49)  
**Owner:** Augustine Ozor &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Due Date:** 2025-12-15

**Description**  
The penetration test executive summary provided during due diligence is over 12 months old, which does not meet the PCI DSS Requirement 11.3 expectation of annual penetration testing. Vendor has been asked for an updated report or a committed testing date within 90 days; current pen-test evidence will be made a condition of contract renewal.

---

## 3. Incomplete Sub-Processor Disclosure

**Category:** ![Process Gap](https://img.shields.io/badge/category-Process%20Gap-e36209) &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Severity:** ![Medium](https://img.shields.io/badge/severity-Medium-dbab09) &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Status:** ![In Progress](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-In%20Progress-dbab09)  
**Owner:** Augustine Ozor &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Due Date:** 2025-11-15

**Description**  
PaySecure was unable to provide a complete list of sub-processors involved in payment processing, nor a documented process for assessing and monitoring fourth-party risk (SOC 2 CC9.2). Vendor is being asked to provide a full, current sub-processor list and their due-diligence methodology, with a contractual right to notification of material sub-processor changes going forward.
